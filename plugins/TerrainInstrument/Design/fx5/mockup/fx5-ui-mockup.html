The last three — Bode · Utility · Splitter

The three cards, their cores LIFTED from the plugin (one copy of the code), audible on worklet mirrors of the certified engines. Press Start audio, then Play. Hear follows whichever card you touch. The spectrum under every card is what you are hearing. Bode: drag the dot on the rail = Shift (white ghost = where the partials were, purple = where they went), wheel on it = Fdbk, double-click = zero, right-click = Up · Ring · Down. Splitter: drag a crossover node, wheel over a band = that lane's gain, right-click a band = Mute · Solo · Flip · Add a device here, the + drops a device into that band. Utility: the buttons are the card. Knobs drag up/down, double-click resets.
