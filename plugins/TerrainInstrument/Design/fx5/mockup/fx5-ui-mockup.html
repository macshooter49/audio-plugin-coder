The last three — Bode · Utility · Splitter

The three cards, their cores LIFTED from the plugin (one copy of the code), audible on worklet mirrors of the certified engines. Press Start audio, then Play. Hear follows whichever card you touch. The spectrum under every card is what you are hearing. Bode: drag the dot on the rail = Shift — the white ghost is where the partials were, the purple is where they went, and the streams between them run in the shift's direction (feedback keeps them going); wheel on the dot = Fdbk, double-click = zero, right-click = Up · Ring · Down. Splitter: click a band to select it (the hairline), the chassis pills Mute · Solo · Flip act on it; drag a crossover node; wheel over a band = that lane's gain; right-click a band = the menu; the + drops a device into that band. Utility (fb450, the channel strip): the card draws the two rails, L and R, through Flip → Haas → Mono → Width/Pan → Swap, lit by what leaves on each; the lamps under it are the switches. The back panel is eight knobs, no dropdowns — High Pass · Low Pass · Bass · Air · Mono Below · Rotate · Haas · Drive — every one audible on a mono source. Knobs drag up/down, double-click resets.
