## 0. The one-paragraph answer

Serum 2's Spectral oscillator is a **per-voice STFT (FFT-bin) resynthesizer of a sample**: it analyses a dropped sample (or a wavetable, or a PNG) into frames, plays the frames back with a Scan/loop read-head decoupled from pitch, then applies **bin-domain** operations per hop — a drawable spectral filter mask, Lo/Hi frequency markers with 4th-order Butterworth edges, Phase Lock and Transients toggles, and two chained warp slots that reach **88 modes** of which **34 are spectral-only** (Detune, Smear, Spread, Harmonics, Sub-harmonics, Gate, Robotize, Shift, Mirror, Peak Follow / Oct ±12 / Hmx ±12 / Harm Sweep, Shepard ×2, Comb, Pitch Blend, Pitch Shift, Phase Twist, Formant, Mask ×6, Vocode ×6). It is Serum 2's most CPU-expensive engine by Xfer's own admission. Terrain's **Geode/Resynth (Engine::SPEC = 3)** is a *partial-domain* SMS resynthesizer: 96 analysed / 64 active sine partials per voice, noise residual analysed but **not rendered**, 640-partial global budget — which is precisely why it reads as "turning anything into a synth". Terrain's other spectral pieces (SpectralMorph's 8 modes, the partial window / Lo-Hi cuts, Blur, the Blend baker, the deferred Stellate shaper) are all **offline bakes or global-mode shapers**, not per-voice real-time spectral processing. The honest gap is *fidelity on non-tonal material + bin-domain warps + cross-oscillator Mask/Vocode*. Two builds are possible: **A** a real per-voice STFT/OLA engine (Serum-class; CPU is affordable — estimated 0.1–0.4 % of a core per unison-voice-channel on vDSP, the same order as Geode measures today — and most of the analysis/FFT/mask/cut code already exists), or **B** extend Geode with Serum's warps as partial-array ops (cheap, fast to ship, but it cannot escape the "it's a synth" ceiling because the noise/transient half of every sound is thrown away by construction). Recommendation: **A, as a new engine slot, phased; keep Geode as Resynth.** SoundFont: Serum 2 itself does **not** load SF2 (`[M2 p.60]`); build the SFZ zone player first and treat SF2 as a 1-week import converter (TinySoundFont, MIT) on top of it, not as an engine.

## Part 1 — Serum 2's oscillator engines, precisely (v2.0–2.1.4)

### 1.1 The five engines

`[M2 p.14]`: "Wavetable, Multisample, Sample, Granular, Spectral" — any of OSC A/B/C can be any of the five. The binary confirms the enum and the architecture: `kOsc_WT = 0, kOsc_MultiSample, kOsc_Sample, kOsc_Granular, kOsc_Spectral, kOsc_Noise, kOsc_Sub` and **one `WarpOscillator` template instantiated over five state types** (`WarpOscControllerI16SpectralOscStateE`, `…I19MultiSampleOscStateE`, …) — warp is a layer over any source, not a wavetable feature `[11-SERUM2 §1, lines 60-75]`. Every engine has **two chained warp slots** each with mode + depth + Var `[11-SERUM2 §2.1]`. Sources: [Xfer product page](https://xferrecords.com/products/serum-2), [What's New PDF](https://static.xferrecords.com/Serum%202%20What's%20New.pdf) (extracted to `scratchpad/whatsnew.txt:166-215`).

### 1.2 SPECTRAL — what it actually is

**Source.** A sample (the same sample already loaded in Sample/Granular carries over), factory tonal/non-tonal presets, a Serum wavetable loaded "as a sample", or `Load Sample` `[M2 p.106]`. Press coverage adds PNG image import ([futureproof deep dive](https://futureproofmusicschool.com/blog/serum-2-spectral-mode-a-producers-deep-dive-into-the-dankest-new-feature), [productionmusiclive](https://www.productionmusiclive.com/blogs/news/how-to-use-spectral-synthesis-in-serum-2) "import an image for curious results"). The manual's own definition `[M2 p.105]`: "generates sound by analyzing and manipulating the frequency spectrum of a sound, breaking it down into its individual frequency components or partials … Note: This method can be CPU intensive". Marketing: "realtime resynthesis of samples at the harmonic level, and transient detection processing similar to that found in advanced timestretching algorithms" ([xferrecords.com](https://xferrecords.com/products/serum-2)).

**It is an STFT, not a sine bank.** The manual's Phase Lock / Transients text is explicit `[M2 p.118]`: "Phase Lock — Adjust the **FFT phases** to minimize the audible phase change **between FFT blocks** … less 'smeared' sound"; "Transients — Preserves transients that would otherwise be smeared by **FFT processing**". The 2.0.17/2.0.21 changelogs talk about "filling FFT buffer from wrong side of loop" ([changelog gist](https://gist.github.com/0xdevalias/a537a59d1389d5aed3bc63b544c70c8d)). The binary exposes `kParamPhaseLock, kParamTransients, kParamLoHiIsPost, kParamLoHiIsSmooth` `[11-SERUM2 §2.1 line 102-104]`. **FFT size / hop / window are undocumented anywhere** — the project's forensics could not recover them `[11-SERUM2 §8.1]`.

**Display and read-head.** Spectrogram display (frequency vertical, time horizontal), drag to zoom `[M2 p.107]`; sample Start/End markers; **Lo/Hi frequency markers** dragged at the right of the spectrogram, modulatable, with two right-click options `[M2 p.108]`: **Smooth** = "fourth-order Butterworth filter at the low and high frequency boundaries", **Post Warp** = "Apply the low/high filtering after processing spectral warps". **SCAN** knob = speed + direction; Range ±200 % (default) / ±400 % / ±800 %; Reverse; **Key Track** (scan rate follows note pitch); Lock Scan Rate to Tempo; **Sample Length to BPM** (SCAN becomes RATE in beats/bars — drum loops stay in tempo while pitched by the keyboard) `[M2 p.117]`. Loop menu shared with Sample mode + **Manual mode**: the playhead becomes a red X|Y dot, SCAN drives its horizontal position, notes do not scan, any mod source can be dropped on the dot; the Y axis can drive Level / Warp / Warp 2 / Spec Flt Cutoff / Spec Flt Wet-Dry / Freq Lo / Freq Hi `[M2 pp.110, 115-116]`. Slicing works in Spectral mode (same as Sample) `[M2 p.72 ref, 3091]`.

**Frequency-domain filtering.** **CUT** = spectral filter cutoff `[M2 p.118]`; **FILTER** = a drawable spectral mask (breakpoints, per-segment curvature, multi-select, grid snap, factory presets, saveable) *or a wavetable used as the mask* (not editable then) `[M2 pp.118-121]`; **MIX** = wet/dry of the spectral filter `[M2 p.122]`. Binary params: `kParamSpecFltShift, kParamSpecFltWetDry, kParamFreqLo, kParamFreqHi` `[11-SERUM2 line 102]` — i.e. the mask can be *shifted* along frequency (Spec Flt Shift is a Y-axis target too).

**The spectral warp list — 88 modes, 34 spectral-only** `[11-SERUM2 §3, lines 218-268; BIN 14804335]`. UI labels recovered from the binary's display-name table; behaviours are the forensics author's `[INFERRED]` reading, **not** measured (Spectral mode is switched by a host message, not an automatable param, so the harness could not drive it — `[11-SERUM2 §8.2]`):

| # | Label | Inferred behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detune +/- | per-partial frequency offset off the harmonic grid |
| 2 | Smear | magnitude/phase blur across bins/time |
| 3 | Spread +/- | stretch/compress partial index about a pivot |
| 4 | Harmonics | synthesise integer multiples of detected partials |
| 5 | Sub harm. | integer divisors |
| 6 | Gate | zero bins below a threshold |
| 7 | Robotize | zero all phases each frame (pitch = frame rate) |
| 8 | Shift | linear Hz shift of all bins (inharmonic) |
| 9 | Mirror | reflect spectrum about a pivot bin |
| 10 | Peak Follow | keep energy only at tracked peaks |
| 11-14 | Peak Oct ±12 / Peak Hmx ±12 | copy tracked peaks an octave / a harmonic index up/down |
| 15 | Harm Sweep | sweep which harmonic the peaks map onto |
| 16-17 | Shepard W / Shepard ? | Shepard-tone comb; second label unrecoverable |
| 18 | Comb | periodic comb over bin index |
| 19-20 | Pitch Blend / Pitch Shift | legacy vs 2.0.19's "new improved" bin-remap pitch shift (changelog) |
| 21 | Phase Twist | progressive phase rotation vs bin (= our Disperse) |
| 22 | Formant | move the spectral envelope, hold partial frequencies |
| 23-28 | Mask (B/C, Noise, Sub, Filter 1, Filter 2) | per-bin multiply/min against another source's spectrum |
| 29-34 | Voc (B/C, Noise, Sub, Filter 1, Filter 2) | vocoder: this osc's envelope replaced by the other source's |

Modes 35–87 are the shared tail every engine has: LPF/HPF, 14 distortions, FM/FMX/FMP/PD/AM/RM × {B, C, Noise, Sub, Filter 1, Filter 2} + self-PD `[11-SERUM2 line 227-234]`. Press corroboration of the spectral-only family: "spread partials, boost harmonics, gate frequencies below a threshold, twist phases and even apply masks or vocoding from other oscillators" ([Sonic Weaponry](https://sonic-weaponry.com/blogs/free-production-tutorials-and-resources/serum-2-released)); "Detune +/-, Bend +/-, Smear, Spread … harmonics, subharmonics, and comb" ([futureproof](https://futureproofmusicschool.com/blog/serum-2-spectral-mode-a-producers-deep-dive-into-the-dankest-new-feature)). *The names Erode / Tilt / Stretch / Blur that the question listed are not Serum 2 spectral warps* — Tilt/Erode are Terrain vocabulary.

**Unison.** Full unison block, same as the other engines `[M2 pp.112-114]`: voices count, MODE (Linear / Super / Exp / Inv / Random), STACK, DETUNE, BLEND (75 % default), WIDTH, RANGE, **SPAN** (fixed start offset per voice), **START** (random start offset), WARP 1 / WARP 2 spread. "Unison causes Serum to generate multiple voices, raising CPU usage. The color of the UNISON field changes … as a reminder" `[M2 p.112]` — i.e. unison = N full STFT voices, no constant-cost trick.

**Musical use.** Snares/vocal chops → basses and pads by scanning/freezing; drawing the mask as a tonal EQ that rides the note; Manual mode + LFO on the X|Y dot as a scrubber; image import; Switch to Wavetable to bake the result `[M2 p.122]`. Xfer's own advice for CPU ([monosounds CPU guide](https://monosounds.studio/serum-2-cpu-optimization/)): "Spectral is Serum 2's most expensive engine … resynthesizing hundreds of partials in real time … use Resample to Oscillator to bake it into a wavetable".

**CPU character.** Steve Duda on the [Xfer forum](https://xferrecords.com/forums/general/serum-2-performance-issues/page-2): "spectral synthesis do take some amount of CPU, this gets compounded with stereo files, polyphony, and unison on top should probably be avoided (with polyphony)". Ranking from cheapest to priciest per [monosounds](https://monosounds.studio/serum-2-oscillator-types/): Sample, Multisample, Wavetable, Spectral, Granular.

### 1.3 MULTISAMPLE — what it is, and SFZ / SF2

`[M2 pp.59-67]`: "an array of samples … played across a range of pitches, intensities, and articulations … selects the appropriate sample based on the MIDI input — evaluating both the pitch and velocity". Loads factory instruments or **`Load SFZ`**; SFZ "map audio samples to specific notes, velocities, or other MIDI parameters … dynamic layers, key switches, **round robins**, and other articulations … filters, envelopes, LFOs" `[M2 p.60]`. **"Serum does not natively support SoundFont 2 (SF2) files. There are, however, multiple open source applications that can convert files from SF2 to SFZ."** `[M2 p.60]`. Display shows the zone map, the played zone lights, click shows the last note's waveform `[M2 p.61]`. Envelope pane: DELAY (BPM-syncable) / A / H / D / S / R with an **OVERRIDE** button over the SFZ's own envelope ("per note, per group, or global") `[M2 pp.62-63]`; VEL TRACK; RAND (start phase). **TIMBRE** = "inversely adjusts mapped samples to pitch" (higher notes play lower-mapped zones — a timbre-shift trick) `[M2 p.64]`. Unison (same block, no SPAN/START), Warp 1/2, Pan, Level. `Switch to Single Sample` (last note → Sample mode) and from there `Switch to Wavetable` `[M2 p.67]`. 2.0.21 fixed "multisample loop mode not enabled for samples with loop metadata" and RAM blow-up on high-sample-count presets (changelog gist). Which SFZ opcodes are honoured is **not documented** anywhere I found.

## Part 2 — What Terrain ALREADY has in the spectral domain

### 2.1 The Engine enum and what "SPEC" means today

`SynthVoice.h:53`: `enum class Engine : int { WT = 0, SAMP = 1, GRAN = 2, SPEC = 3, FM = 4, HARM = 5, MODAL = 6 };` with the APVTS `StringArray { "WT", "SAMP", "GRAN", "SPEC", "FM", "HARM", "MODAL" }` at `PluginProcessor.cpp:2389` (×4 oscs). Slot 5 was the never-exposed NOISE engine, remapped to HARMONIC (`SynthVoice.h:50-52`). **SPEC = Geode**, UI-named "Resynth" (`GeodeEngine.h:3-6`). `kMaxUnison = 16` (`SynthVoice.h:55`), `kSynthVoiceCount = 96` (`PluginProcessor.h:1543`). Per voice there are `std::array<tw::GeodeEngine, kMaxUnison> geodeEngA_…D_` — 64 statically allocated engines per voice (`SynthVoice.h:6632`).

### 2.2 Geode / Resynth (Engine::SPEC) — the partial-domain resynth

- Analysis: offline STFT, `kWin = 2048`, `kHop = 512`, `kBins = 1025`, `kMaxFrames = 256` (long samples **subsampled** by `stride`), `kMaxPartials = 96`, `kNoiseBands = 16` (`GeodeEngine.h:43-55`, `:223-262`). Peak detect → birth/death tracker → 16-band noise envelope per frame. Frame = `{ratio[96], amp[96], noise[16]}` (`:64-69`).
- **The residual is analysed but not rendered**: "residual noise band envelope (kept for analysis stability; not rendered)" (`GeodeEngine.h:63-64`). This is the single biggest reason it "turns anything into a synth".
- Render: per-voice sine bank, LUT, "~1 sine-osc PER PARTIAL PER SAMPLE" (`:45-46`), measured **2.65 ns/partial-sample** (`mem:terrain-instrument-geode-spectral-waterfall-viz.md:134`). Governor: `kMaxActive = 64` per voice, `kUnisonFloor = 10`, and the shared `kGeodePartialBudget = 640` "all SPEC + HARM voices/unison combined" (`GeodeEngine.h:47-49`, `PluginProcessor.h:1859`), per-block quota reset (`mem:project-terrain-geode-resynthesis-engine.md:261-268`).
- **Constant-cost unison**: anchor prepares the sculpted bank once, siblings adopt it and render only their detuned banks; active partials divided by ⌈√N⌉ (`SynthVoice.h:7212-7232`, `GeodeEngine.h:438`).
- Measured CPU after the rs2 governor: 1-note×16-unison **2.8 %**, 4-note×16 **8.8 %**, STRETCH pad **9.2 %**, absolute worst 32-note×16 **12.3 %** of a core; before: 40-61 % (`mem:…waterfall-viz.md:144`). `GeodeEngine_test.cpp:230` carries a permanent `[cpu-ceiling]` gate; `GeodeEngine_cpubench.cpp:76-116` is the harness.
- Controls (`ParameterIDs.hpp:972-1000`): Scan · Stretch · Sieve (gate, 6 modes incl. Cloak) · Cut (LP/HP) · Shape (tune overtones to harmonics, 11 targets) · Drive (6 modes, 3 of which grow *child partials* = Harmonics/diff-tones) · Quality · Formant (true-envelope) · Tilt · Crush · Start · Melt (temporal smear) · Formant-Keep · Loop. Pitched to the key, never detuned (`GeodeEngine.h:8-24`).
- Deferred from geo1: the partial visualizer, and **the drawable spectral-filter mask extension card** (`mem:project-terrain-geode-resynthesis-engine.md:303-305`). Wavetable-source analysis (`buildFromWave`, `GeodeEngine.h:266`) exists but is not wired ("Resynth is sample-only").

### 2.3 SpectralMorph.h — the per-osc wavetable spectral MORPH (offline bake)

`SpectralMorph.h:39-51`: `enum class SpectralMode { None, HarmonicStretch, InharmonicStretch, Vocode, Smear, RandomAmplitudes, DataCompress, SpectralPhaser, Disperse, Count }` — 8 live modes (7 + fb467's Disperse). `apply()` is pure and amount-parameterised (`:87-106`), operates on a `WavetableSpec` partial list, output goes to `Wavetable::buildFromSpec` (`:5-9`). **It is a table bake on the message thread**: `PluginProcessor.cpp:1356` `slot.buf[target].buildFromSpec (tw::SpectralMorph::apply (*srcSpec, mode, amount, lo, hi))`, atomic-published per osc (`mem:terrain-instrument-spectral-morph-complete-7-modes.md:121-131`); a bake is ~2.3 ms after the vDSP swap (`PluginProcessor.h:1592`, `mem:terrain-instrument-spectral-overpass-fb467.md:132`). Params: `SYN_OSC_x_SPECTRAL_TYPE/AMT/LO/HI` (`ParameterIDs.hpp:570-576`); LO/HI became **4th-order Butterworth cuts in harmonic number** (the order Serum uses on its spectral Lo/Hi markers) (`SpectralMorph.h:62-77`). `kMaxPartials` 96→512 (`Wavetable.h:56-70`). The design note says per-voice application "is a drop-in" (`SpectralMorph.h:17-20`) — true for the *math*, false for the *cost*: a per-voice bake was measured at ~25 % per modulated voice in the Phase-11 fork (`mem:terrain-instrument-phase-11bcdg-mega-session-2026-06-03.md:55`).

The old per-sample "spectral FX rack" is still in the voice (`SynthVoice.h:2520 setSpectral`, `:4305 if (! spectralBypassA_)`) but `spectralBypassA_ = true` by default (`:7657`) and no caller in the processor — dead code.

### 2.4 The wavetable harmonic pipeline (fb523/fb530)

`FrameSpec::kMaxHarmonics = 1024` (`Wavetable.h:37`), `kFrameSize = 2048`, **61-level superset mip ladder** (`:380-381`), `kMaxFrames = 256` imported; `toSpec()` = forward FFT of any loaded table into a 16-frame spec (`:626-640`); `WtFft.h` = vDSP `vDSP_fft_zripD` real double bake transform, **2,548 ns per 2048-pt inverse (f32 zrip: 1,249 ns)** with the radix-2 scalar path as non-Apple fallback and gate reference (`WtFft.h:14-30`). The One Law: no generator may carry a harmonic-count ceiling (`Tests/README.md:22`, `harmonic_ceiling_gate.py`). Blur: `renderBlend` Gaussian frame-axis mean built once per block (`Wavetable.h:824-846`) plus the **phase-aligned twin** so blur = the magnitude law with no audio-thread FFT (`Wavetable.h:711-782`, `mem:terrain-instrument-blur-twin-and-cuts-fb469-471.md:192-212`).

### 2.5 HarmonicEngine (Engine::HARM) — the real-time additive engine

"Pure ADDITIVE synthesis: up to 512 partials rendered as a per-voice sine bank … the same proven machinery as the Resynth engine" (`HarmonicEngine.h:5-10`, `harm::kMaxPartials = 512` `:49`). Its SCULPT row already implements KEEL tilt, SPLAY inharmonic stretch, CULL sieves, TIDE ripple, TERRACE dB-quantise, CLANG intermod (`:21-28`). The build bible routes every "per-voice spectral" idea here (`Design/spectral/SPECTRAL-BUILD-BIBLE.md:990-998`).

### 2.6 Blend (offline baker), Stellate (deferred), the sample engine's phase vocoder

- **BlendEngine.h** combines two one-shots offline: STFT, STN split (attack/body/breath), 1-D optimal-transport morph, ISTFT; "Per-voice CPU delta: ZERO" (`BlendEngine.h:3-27`; `mem:project-terrain-blend-engine-scope.md:326-331`).
- **Stellate** (`Source/_deferred/StellateNode.h`, not compiled): a global-mode, MIDI-pitch-locked lock-in-analyser + sine-bank shaper, 12 harmonic shapes, spectral LP/HP in the harmonic domain, AIR residual, FEED; deferred 2026-07-02 per Max ("that can come later") with reactivation steps in `_deferred/README.md:1-40`; `kPoly = 8, kPart = 16` (`StellateNode.h:64-65`). Lesson recorded: resynthesis has no dry mix (`mem:project-terrain-engines-endgame-spectral-modal.md:74-78`).
- **The sample engine already runs a per-voice STFT phase vocoder**: `SYN_OSC_x_SAMPLE_STRETCH / FORMANT (+ _MODE)` (`ParameterIDs.hpp:750-761`) engage `tw::WarpProcessor sampleWarpA_…D_` (`SynthVoice.h:6609`, one per osc per voice, lazily allocated `Warp/WarpProcessor.h:7-10`) wrapping vendored **Signalsmith Stretch** (`_tools/signalsmith-stretch/signalsmith-stretch.h`, MIT) in `presetCheaper` = 100 ms block / 40 ms hop / `splitComputation=true` (`Warp/SignalsmithEngine.h:31-45`). It exposes `setTransposeFactor`, `setFreqMap(std::function)`, `setFormantFactor/Base` (`signalsmith-stretch.h:107-133`). Engaging it was a documented CPU **cliff** (`mem:terrain-instrument-cpu-optimizations.md:13-18`), now flattened; it cannot run ×16 unison (`mem:terrain-instrument-sample-engine-v2-unison-timbral-keytrack.md:37`).
- Real-time FFT elsewhere: `SpectrumAnalyzer.h:24-26` (juce::dsp::FFT 4096, hop 1024, display only, now off the audio thread per fb488). Inventory of the three hand-rolled radix-2 FFTs with three normalisation conventions and the rule "a fourth is a defect": `Design/spectral/00-INVENTORY.md:467-485`.

### 2.7 The sample engine's multisample state

- Synth-page **Sample engine is single-sample** per osc: pitch-accurate cubic-Hermite playback, bipolar SCAN, 5 loop modes, region + inner loop, X-Fade, SPRAY (`SampleEngine.h:1-25`), keytrack/unison/timbral from v2 (memory). No zone map, no velocity layers, no per-note RR.
- The older Mix-page **4 LAYERS** carry `keyZoneMin/Max`, `velocityZoneMin/Max`, `probabilityWeight` (`LayerState.h:52-60`) and trigger modes LAYER / ROUND-ROBIN / RANDOM / KEYTRACK / VELOCITY with `roundRobinPos`, `rrShuffle` (`PluginProcessor.h:1364-1377`) — a 4-zone multisampler at the layer level, not per-osc.
- Max's multisample vision (deferred, post-release): four realism layers (density, velocity layers, round robins, sustain loops + release samples), **SFZ as the "cheat"**, licence law (CC0 clear, CC-BY credit, NC no, unknown-origin SF2 = lawsuit bait), build = "reader-not-library: an SFZ-style zone player ON TOP of the existing sample engine"; RR logic already owned (Robin flow mode) (`mem:project-terrain-multisample-vision-sfz.md:383-397`). The per-note playhead follower already handles one line per sounding voice (`mem:terrain-instrument-multi-sample-follower.md:415-420`).

### 2.8 The CPU frame every decision sits in

Hard rule (Max, 2026-07-03): least-CPU architecture that still sounds great; "what does this cost at 10 held notes?"; benchmark before/after in ns/sample (`mem:feedback-terrain-cpu-friendly-hard-rule.md:11-27`). Current floor: **idle DSP with ZERO voices = 19 % of one core** (`gather 13 | fx+master 6`, blk 512 @ 48 k), UI build 15 % of a core while a note is held; the CPU overpass is queued after the Big Three (`mem:project-terrain-roadmap-2026-09.md:28-30, 56`). Idle per-call cost was flattened for FL's 45-sample blocks (`mem:terrain-instrument-windows-port-fb480-497.md:22-27`). Memory is also a live constraint: Mac instance reads 2.2 GB vs 925 MB on Windows, target ≤ 1 GB (`mem:project-terrain-roadmap-2026-09.md:14`) — static `kMaxUnison` engine arrays per voice are part of that.

## Part 3 — Gap analysis, reuse, two architectures, CPU, plan

### 3.1 What a Serum-2-class Spectral engine adds that Terrain cannot do now

| Capability | Serum 2 Spectral | Terrain today | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faithful playback of ANY sample decoupled in time and pitch (voice, snare, loop) | STFT/OLA, Phase Lock, Transients `[M2 p.118]` | Geode: ≤96 analysed / ≤64 active sines, **no noise rendered**, ≤256 frames (`GeodeEngine.h:43-55, 63`) | **Structural.** A breath, a snare, a crowd becomes a whistle-tone. This is Max's "it's a synth" |
| Per-voice, per-hop spectral *processing* with morphable warps | 2 slots × 88 modes, Var, unison spread of warp `[11-SERUM2 §3]` | WT: offline bake, one table shared by all voices (`SpectralMorph.h:14-16`); Geode: 12 partial-domain knobs | Per-voice bin-domain ops do not exist |
| Drawable spectral mask + Lo/Hi markers + Post-Warp order + Spec Flt Shift | `[M2 pp.108, 118-121]` | LO/HI cuts on the wavetable morph only (harmonic number, not Hz) (`SpectralMorph.h:62-77`); Geode Cut LP/HP; mask card deferred | Mask editor + shift not built |
| Cross-oscillator Mask / Vocode (from B/C, Noise, Sub, Filter 1/2) | 12 modes | none | none (see 3.2 — cheaper for us than for Serum) |
| Manual X|Y scrub mode, Sample-Length-to-BPM, scan Key Track | `[M2 pp.110, 117]` | Geode Scan/Start/Stretch, Sample engine SCAN | Small |
| Image (PNG) import | yes | no | Trivial once frames are magnitude arrays |
| Switch to Wavetable | `[M2 p.122]` | `Wavetable::toSpec` exists (`Wavetable.h:632`), import path exists | Small |

What Terrain has that Serum's Spectral does not: pitch-invariant "never detunes" resynth with SHAPE tuning of overtones (`GeodeEngine.h:8-24`), spectral Drive children, the 640 budget governor with constant-cost unison, the Blend baker, and the fact that WT/HARM/SPEC oscillators are **spectral-native** (their spectra are known arrays, no FFT needed to read them).

### 3.2 What can be REUSED (by file)

- **Analysis**: `GeodeAnalyzer::analyzeSample` STFT loop (`GeodeEngine.h:223-262`) — keep the raw `mag[1025]` (and phase) per hop instead of peak-picking; `detectF0`, `hann()`, the frame subsampling `stride`. The 2048/512 numbers are Serum-class already.
- **Transform**: `WtFft.h`'s vDSP real transform (`WtFft.h:14-30`) — the 1.25 µs f32 inverse is exactly what a per-hop OLA needs. Rule: do not add a fourth FFT (`Design/spectral/00-INVENTORY.md:482-485`); route through `wtfft::`.
- **Cuts / mask law**: the 4th-order Butterworth `|H|` formulas (`SpectralMorph.h:66-70`) reapplied in Hz bins; the smoothstep partial-window blend that is already mutation-gated (`Tests/spec_cert.cpp`).
- **Warps that are already written as partial ops** and port to bins or partials: Harmonic/Inharmonic stretch, Vocode formants, Smear, Random amps, DataCompress, Spectral phaser, Disperse (`SpectralMorph.h:181-400`); Geode's Formant (Röbel true-envelope), Tilt, Sieve modes, Shape, Melt, Drive children (`GeodeEngine.h` applySculpt); HARM's KEEL/SPLAY/CULL/TERRACE/CLANG (`HarmonicEngine.h:21-28`).
- **Read-head + UI**: Geode's Scan/Stretch/Start/Loop head and the 128×64 spectral-image strip viz (`mem:…waterfall-viz.md rs6`), Sample/Granular's loop header selector, the per-voice playhead follower.
- **Budget pattern**: the per-block quota + keep-loudest thinning (`mem:project-terrain-geode-resynthesis-engine.md:261-268`), `GeodeEngine_cpubench.cpp` as the harness template, the `[cpu-ceiling]` gate.
- **Publish pattern**: `GeodeSlot` double-buffer + atomic publish per osc, analysis in `timerCallback` (`mem:project-terrain-geode-resynthesis-engine.md:287-290`).
- **Per-voice STFT plumbing** (if Signalsmith is used as the core): `WarpProcessor`'s lazy allocation (`Warp/WarpProcessor.h:7-14`), `presetCheaper`/`splitComputation` learning (`mem:terrain-instrument-cpu-optimizations.md:15-18`).

### 3.3 Architecture A — per-voice STFT/OLA engine (true spectral)

**Shape.** New `Engine::SPECTRAL = 7` (keep SPEC=3 as Resynth; the string array at `PluginProcessor.cpp:2389` grows by one — the fb373 "index trap" applies: all four `<select>`s and `StringArray`s must grow in one commit, `mem:terrain-wavetable-harmonics-were-never-generated-fb523.md:277-286`). Store: per osc, frames of `{mag[1025], phase[1025]}` f32 at hop 512 (a 3 s sample = 281 frames × 8 KB = 2.3 MB; cap by frames-on-disk not 256). Per voice per unison: read-head → frame interpolation (mag lerp, phase from the phase-locked vocoder) → **warp slot(s)** (bin ops) → mask × Lo/Hi (Smooth/Post-Warp order) → pitch to note (peak-locked bin remap, ratio = f_note/f0; formant-preserving variant = Geode's Formant-Keep) → iFFT (vDSP f32) → Hann OLA. Transients toggle = detect onset frames at analysis time and, on those hops, bypass the phase lock and shorten the window (that is what "transient detection like advanced timestretchers" means).

**CPU (estimate, not yet measured — the bench must be built first per the rule).** Per hop (512 samples = 10.67 ms of audio) per voice per channel: iFFT 1.25 µs (`WtFft.h:22`) + window/OLA ~1 µs + frame interp + phase advance over 1025 bins ~3-5 µs + one warp ~5-15 µs + mask/cuts ~2 µs ≈ **12-25 µs/hop → 1.1-2.3 ms per second → 0.11-0.23 % of a core per voice-channel**; stereo store ×2, unison ×N (no √N trick — but Geode's anchor/adopt split still applies: the mask/warp/interp is shared across unison siblings, only the pitch-remap + iFFT is per sibling, so siblings cost ~4 µs/hop ≈ 0.04 %). Worked case: 8 notes × 4 unison × stereo ≈ 8 × (2×0.2 + 6×0.04) ≈ **5 %** of a core; 8 × 16 uni ≈ 8 × (0.4 + 30×0.04) ≈ **13 %**. That is the same order as Geode measures today (1×16 = 2.8 %, 4×16 = 8.8 %) and far below the pre-governor 40-60 % that got Geode in trouble. Attack latency is intrinsic: 2048 window = 43 ms smear at 48 k unless Transients shortens it. Memory per voice-unison: 2 × 2048 OLA + 1025 phases ≈ 20 KB — **must be lazily pooled**, not `std::array<…, kMaxUnison>` per voice (6144 instances × 20 KB = 123 MB for one osc row; the 2.2 GB Mac RSS is already the complaint).

**Variant A2 — Signalsmith Stretch as the STFT core.** It is already per-voice in the tree, MIT, proven RT-safe, does phase-locked pitch shift + formant preservation, exposes `setFreqMap` (= Shift / Spread / Mirror / Detune for free) and `setFormantFactor` (`signalsmith-stretch.h:107-133`). Missing a magnitude hook (mask, gate, tilt, smear, comb) — a small patch on the vendored header where `processSpectrumSteps` runs (`:316`). Cons: 100 ms block / 40 ms hop in `presetCheaper` = warps update at 25 Hz and 100 ms attack smear; no transient mode; unison ×16 impossible (memory). Good enough for a **prototype week** to test whether Max hears "a spectral engine"; not the ship architecture.

### 3.4 Architecture B — "spectral oscillator" = Geode + Serum's warps as partial-array ops

Add a warp slot to Geode's per-block `applySculpt` chain: Shift (ratio → (ratio·f0 + k)/f0), Spread± (pivot stretch — SPLAY exists), Detune±, Sub-harmonics / Harmonics (Drive children exist), Gate (Sieve exists), Comb, Mirror, Peak Oct/Hmx ±12 (trivial on a partial list), Harm Sweep, Formant (exists). Cost: O(P ≤ 64) per block per anchor voice — effectively free; ships in days on the existing 2×6 UI pattern. **Cross-osc Mask/Vocode is cheaper for Terrain than for Serum**: a WT osc's spectrum is its `WavetableSpec` frame (known), HARM's is its partial array, SPEC's is its bank — a per-partial envelope lookup, no FFT. Only SAMP/GRAN/FM/Noise sources would need an actual FFT.
**What B cannot fix**: the residual is not rendered (`GeodeEngine.h:63`), 96/64 partials, 256 frames, the STRETCH→partials-pinned-at-max CPU behaviour, and phase-domain warps (Phase Twist, Robotize) are inaudible or meaningless on a sine bank. Rendering the noise residual is a real upgrade inside B (16 band-passes on filtered noise per voice, cheap, and the envelopes are already analysed) but it is still "a synth with a noise layer", not the sample.

### 3.5 Recommendation and phased plan

**Build A as a new engine, keep Geode as Resynth, do B's cheap warps for Geode only if Max wants them there.** Reason: the complaint is fidelity, and only an STFT gives it; the CPU is affordable by estimate and Geode's governor pattern bounds it; most of the code is reuse.

- **Phase 0 (2-3 days) — the number first.** Standalone harness (`SpectralEngine_cpubench.cpp`, clone of `GeodeEngine_cpubench.cpp:76-116`): per-hop cost of iFFT+OLA+remap+one warp on vDSP at 2048/512, 1 / 8 / 32 voices, uni 1/4/16. Go/no-go against "well under ~25 % worst case" (`GeodeEngine_cpubench.cpp:154`). Also A2 prototype on `sampleWarpA_` with a magnitude hook to let Max hear the character.
- **Phase 1 (the minimum that sounds like a spectral engine, ~2-3 weeks):** new slot; analysis store (mag+phase per hop, reuse `analyzeSample`'s loop); read-head with Scan (range/reverse/key-track), Start, loop modes, Manual X|Y; phase-locked pitch to note + Phase Lock and Transients toggles; Lo/Hi markers (Smooth, Post-Warp); CUT + MIX; **one warp slot with 8 modes**: Shift, Spread±, Smear, Gate, Comb, Formant, Harmonics, Mirror; unison with anchor/adopt; the 640-style shared *hop budget* (thin unison siblings first, never the anchor); spectrogram viz (reuse the Geode image strip); `[cpu-ceiling]` gate + AU path gate (`feedback-verify-the-path-not-just-the-engine`). Everything measured against the same sample rendered by Geode and by Serum 2 (the AU host from fb523 exists) on centroid / rolloff / noise-floor, not level.
- **Phase 2:** drawable mask editor (the deferred Geode extension card), Spec Flt Shift, second warp slot + Var + unison warp spread, Peak family + Harm Sweep + Phase Twist (= Disperse) + Sub-harm, Mask/Vocode from WT/HARM/SPEC (partial-domain) then from SAMP/GRAN/Noise (FFT), Sample-Length-to-BPM, PNG import (magnitude frames from pixels), Switch-to-Wavetable via `toSpec`.
- **Phase 3:** Shepard, Robotize, Pitch Blend/Shift variants, Terrain-only extras (Drive children, Melt, Shape tuning) ported into the bin domain; the "Faithful" mode for Geode if still wanted.

### 3.6 SoundFont (.sf2) engine — one paragraph

Serum 2 does not load SF2 at all — only SFZ, and its manual points users at SF2→SFZ converters (`[M2 p.60]`), so an SF2 loader would be a differentiator only in the sense that Serum declined it. Feasibility: **TinySoundFont** ([github](https://github.com/schellingb/TinySoundFont)) is MIT, a single header (~1,300 lines of implementation), supports the SF2 generators, ADSR envelopes, sustain/continuous loops, the LPF, key/velocity zones, exclusive class, and renders allocation-free once `tsf_set_max_voices` is set; it lacks SF2 modulators and chorus/reverb sends. **FluidSynth** is LGPL (dynamic-link obligations, a shared lib to ship) — not for a static plugin. sfizz (permissive, [github](https://github.com/sfztools/sfizz)) is the heavyweight SFZ option; SFZero is MIT but JUCE-module-shaped. The trap: using tsf as a *renderer* gives a black-box instrument that bypasses Terrain's per-voice filters, mod matrix, unison, warps and flow engines — the differentiator Max named is "feeding real instruments through the flow engines (glitch/chop/robin a multisampled guitar — nobody does that)" (`mem:project-terrain-multisample-vision-sfz.md:397`). So the right shape is **parser-only**: use tsf (or a 400-line clean-room RIFF/pdta reader — the SF2 spec is public) to turn an .sf2 into in-memory zones `{sample, lokey/hikey, lovel/hivel, rootKey, tune, loop, env}` and hand them to the same **zone player on top of `SampleEngine`** that the SFZ plan already calls for (`mem:…sfz.md:396`). Shared work with the sample engine: everything except the zone map + velocity crossfade + per-note RR (`seq_position`) + release-sample trigger; the RR picker exists in the Mix-page trigger modes (`PluginProcessor.h:1364-1377`) and Robin. Size: SFZ zone player ≈ 1.5-2 weeks (text parser + zone map + RR + release samples + zone-map display + 4-osc plumbing); SF2 import on top ≈ 3-5 days; content is the real cost (GM SF2 banks are 30-300 MB+, provenance often shady — licence law in memory). Worth it? **SFZ multisample: yes** (Serum parity, text, curated CC0/CC-BY content). **SF2 as its own engine: no**; as an *import path into the multisample engine*: cheap enough to add once zones exist, and only then.
