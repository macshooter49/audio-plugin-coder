Terrain · synth control redesign — thin & sharp

Four directions, all in a precision-instrument language (Teenage Engineering / Apple / Orchestral Tools): hairline strokes, crisp pointers, depth as a whisper of light — no chunk, no bloom. Each drops into the existing 64-knob system (the arc stays a value-driven conic-gradient; body + pointer are new CSS layers). Drag a knob vertically to feel it; double-click resets. My pick

1 · Hairline

Orchestral Tools / Apple — refined & precise

A 2px arc, a sharp white pointer, a flat dark face with a single 1px top light for the faintest sense of a surface. No glow. Reads instantly, sits quiet, scales razor-clean to tiny sizes.

2 · TE Dot

Teenage Engineering — flat, grid-precise

Completely flat face, no pointer line — a single crisp accent dot rides the rim and is the value. The most "OP-1" of the set: minimal, confident, zero ornament.

3 · Tick

most minimal — near-bodiless

Barely a knob: a hairline outline, a thin value arc, and a short sharp ruler-tick at the position. Maximum restraint — disappears into the panel until you reach for it.

4 · Soft Bevel

Apple-refined — depth, kept thin

For when you want a touch more dimension: a faint domed light across the face (a hint, not a dome) under the same thin pointer + arc. The bridge to your earlier "depth & perspective" without going chunky.
