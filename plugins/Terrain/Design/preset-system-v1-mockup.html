Quick menu — click the preset name. Left column is banks (click one), right column is that bank's presets grouped by type; ‹› step inside the current bank. Browser — single click selects and auditions, double-click or ↵ loads and closes, ↑↓ step, esc closes. Right-click a User preset for rename / delete. The dice picks a random preset from whatever is filtered — narrow first, then roll. Editable — the + at the end of the Type and Style rows adds your own; right-click any chip to rename or remove it, and every preset re-files itself under the new name. Right-click an imported bank to rename or remove it. Search reads names, types, styles, effects, authors and banks; every word you type must match. Carries — the six marks are wavetables · one-shots · IRs · flow cards · LFO shapes · nodes. A preset with nodes is an environment; loading one greys the window and shows the bar.
