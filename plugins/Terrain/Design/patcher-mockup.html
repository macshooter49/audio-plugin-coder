Read it like the plugin. Notes come in on the left, sound leaves on the right. Click empty space to open the browser and add a node · drag a port to another port to patch (legal targets light up; drop on empty space to add a node already wired) · scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan · click a cable then ⌫ to cut it, right-click a cable to mute it · right-click a node for Duplicate / Save as my node / Remove · drag a knob up and down · the power dot on MIDI In starts and stops the notes · the headphone lets you hear the patch · the dice builds a random one. A node with no path to Out goes dim and costs nothing. An oscillator with no notes cable takes them from MIDI In (the dotted stub) — you only draw a notes cable to put an Arp, a Robin or a split in between.
