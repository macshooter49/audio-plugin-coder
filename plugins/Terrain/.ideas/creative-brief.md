# Earcandy - Creative Brief

**Series:** Rack
**Plugin:** Earcandy
**Category:** Granular Texturizer / Generative Soundscape Engine

---

## Hook

Turn any audio into a living, breathing soundscape. Earcandy is a granular texturizer that chops, scatters, and reshapes incoming audio into evolving generative textures — from subtle shimmer to full ambient clouds.

## Description

Earcandy captures incoming audio into a grain buffer and continuously spawns overlapping micro-samples (grains) from that buffer. By adjusting grain size, density, positional scatter (spray), pitch transposition, and feedback recirculation, the user can transform any source — vocals, guitars, synths, field recordings — into rich, evolving soundscapes.

The engine is designed for real-time use: feed it a pad, a vocal phrase, or even percussive hits, and Earcandy will dissolve the source into granular textures that sit beautifully behind a mix or take center stage as generative ambience.

### Core Behavior

- **Grain Engine:** Continuously records incoming audio into a circular buffer. Grains are spawned at configurable intervals, reading from randomized positions within the buffer.
- **Spray Control:** Randomizes where each grain starts reading from within the buffer, creating everything from tight echoes (low spray) to scattered clouds (high spray).
- **Pitch Shifting:** Each grain can be pitch-shifted, enabling shimmer effects (octave up), deep drones (octave down), or detuned textures (subtle shift).
- **Feedback Loop:** Routes processed output back into the grain buffer, causing textures to evolve and build over time. At high settings, creates self-generating soundscapes that persist after the input stops.
- **Simplicity First:** Six knobs. No menus, no pages, no hidden parameters. What you see is what you tweak.

### Use Cases

- Ambient pads from any audio source
- Vocal texturizing and ghostly atmospheres
- Generative background textures for film/game scoring
- Glitchy, scattered rhythmic deconstruction
- Shimmer reverb-like effects via pitch + feedback

## Visual Aesthetic

- **Style:** Minimal, light UI with a slight purple tint
- **Knobs:** Glass knobs — translucent, refractive appearance
- **Layout:** Single row or compact grid of 6 knobs
- **Series Branding:** "Rack" series identity — clean, modular, utilitarian
